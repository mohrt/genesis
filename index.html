TMSS boot ROM

None is needed: the Genesis starts games without one. Later Genesis consoles have this small boot ROM (TMSS), which shows "PRODUCED BY OR UNDER LICENSE FROM SEGA ENTERPRISES LTD." before the game; with one, the console starts in it as those consoles do. It is still under copyright, so none is included: choose or drop your own. It stays in this browser and is never sent anywhere.

TMSS boot ROM Optional

The boot ROM of later consoles. Exactly 2K (2,048 bytes).

Often named tmss.md, bios_MD.bin or GEN_boot.md.

The retrobios collection keeps it as tmss.md in its Mega Drive folder.

Gamepads work as soon as you press a button; the first is player 1, the second player 2.
